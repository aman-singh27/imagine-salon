Our Sanitary Standard.

In modern grooming, absolute purity is not optional. At Imagine Salon Gurugram, we implement surgical-grade hygiene protocols to keep you safe and glowing.

Autoclaved & Sterilized Tools

Every steel clipper, pusher, and scissor undergoes ultrasonic cleaning followed by high-pressure steam autoclave sterilization.

Strict Single-Use Disposables

Every wax strip, bedsheet, nail buffer, file, and styling cape is 100% single-use and disposed of in front of the client.

Clean Air & Station Sanitization

Stations are thoroughly disinfected using professional virucidal sprays before and after every client session.
