VISUAL TRUST EXPERIENCES

Our Triple-Sterilization Standard

Every instrument used on your skin or nails undergoes a meticulous medical-grade cleaning cycle.

01

Ultrasonic Cleaning

All steel instruments are bathed in high-frequency ultrasonic waves with professional surgical detergent to disintegrate micro-particles and chemical residues.

02

High-Pressure Autoclaving

Tools are sealed inside class-B medical grade indicator pouches and heated to 134°C under 2.1 bar steam pressure, killing 100% of bacterial spores and virucidal agents.

03

Pouch Opened Live

Your personalized sterile tool pouch remains fully sealed until you are seated. Your technician breaks the sterile indicator strip live in front of you.
